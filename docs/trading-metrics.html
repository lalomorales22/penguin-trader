📊 What Do These Numbers Mean?

💰 PRICE
$0.0001354
What it is: The current cost to buy ONE token in USD.

Why it matters: This is your entry point. Lower prices can mean more tokens per dollar, but price alone doesn't tell the full story.
Example: With $100, you can buy 738,552 tokens at this price.
💧 LIQUIDITY
$51K
What it is: Total value of crypto locked in the trading pool (like a reserve fund).

Why it matters: Higher liquidity = easier to buy/sell without moving the price. Low liquidity = dangerous!
⚠️ BURRY RULE: Avoid tokens with liquidity under $50K. They're traps waiting to rug pull.
📈 MARKET CAP
$135K
What it is: Total value of ALL tokens in circulation (price × circulating supply).

Why it matters: Shows how "big" the project is. Low market cap = high risk but big upside potential.
Comparison:
• Bitcoin: $1.8 Trillion
• This token: $135K (early!)
🔮 FDV (Fully Diluted Valuation)
$135K
What it is: Market cap if ALL tokens were unlocked and in circulation.

Why it matters: If FDV >> Market Cap, watch out! Tons of tokens could dump later.
Here FDV = Market Cap, meaning all tokens are already circulating. ✅ Good sign!
📊 VOLUME (24H)
$87K
What it is: Total USD traded in the last 24 hours.

Why it matters: High volume = lots of activity and interest. Dead tokens have no volume.
PENGUIN SIGNAL: When volume spikes 3x average, a big move is coming. Watch for divergence!
⚖️ BUY/SELL PRESSURE
254 / 189
What it is: Number of buy transactions vs sell transactions.

Why it matters: More buys = bullish pressure. More sells = bearish pressure.
Current: 254 buys, 189 sells = Bullish (1.34:1 ratio)
💸 BUY/SELL VOLUME
$45K / $42K
What it is: Dollar volume of buys vs sells.

Why it matters: Shows WHERE the money is flowing. Big buy volume = whales accumulating.
BURRY EDGE: If buy volume is high but price isn't rising, it's absorbing sell pressure. Prepare for breakout.
⏱️ TIME FRAMES
What it is: Price change over different time periods (5min, 1h, 6h, 24h).

Why it matters: Shows momentum and trend direction.
Reading this chart:
• 5M: -3.38% (recent dip)
• 1H: -2.54% (pullback)
• 6H: +35.50% (huge pump!)

🎯 How To Use These Metrics for Trading

For Penguin Longs

1. Watch BTC price on main chart

2. When BTC dumps -3% to -8%, scan alts

3. Look for green 6H/24H percentages (+10% to +25%)

4. Check volume ratio: 2-3x = good

5. Check buy/sell pressure: more buys = bullish

6. If all align: LONG with 30-40x leverage

For Burry Shorts

1. Look for massive green percentages (6H > +30%)

2. Check recent 5M/1H: turning red = reversal

3. Volume must be 3x+ average (FOMO spike)

4. Sell pressure increasing (more sells than buys)

5. Need RSI/MACD/ADX/Stochastic data (not shown on basic chart)

6. If 5/5 signals: SHORT with 40-50x leverage

Red Flags to Avoid

🚫 NEVER TRADE IF:
• Liquidity under $50K (rug pull risk)
• Volume under $10K daily (dead coin)
• FDV 10x higher than market cap (unlocks coming)
• Only 20-30 transactions (bots, not real trading)
• Huge spread between buys/sells (wash trading)
